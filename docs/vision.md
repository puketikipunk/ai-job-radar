# Vision

## The problem

Good roles are scattered across company career sites and job boards. Generic alerts produce noise, are often duplicated, and provide little explanation of why a role fits. This is especially costly for people whose experience is technically strong but described in language unfamiliar to a recruiter or a general-purpose job board.

## The product promise

CareerPilot delivers a small, dependable set of new opportunities worth reviewing. Every recommendation should answer three questions quickly: what is the role, where did it come from, and why is it relevant to the user's stated profile.

## Design values

- **Trust over volume:** do not optimise for the largest possible list of jobs.
- **Explainability over opaque ranking:** rule-based recommendations show the factors behind their score.
- **User control:** profiles, sources, and exclusions are configuration, not hard-coded assumptions.
- **Responsible collection:** use approved APIs, feeds, and public pages that permit access; favour company career sources.
- **Privacy by default:** retain the minimum personal data needed and keep secrets outside version control.
- **Progressive capability:** deliver a useful digest first; add AI and workflow assistance only with explicit opt-in.

## Initial audience and profile

The first profile is an Ireland-based technical project/programme professional with engineering, software delivery, Agile, requirements, testing, risk, and stakeholder-management experience. The system is configurable so this profile informs defaults without becoming a permanent product limitation.
